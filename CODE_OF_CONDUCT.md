# Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in BOOTCRAFT a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or education.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, curious, and constructive.
- Explain corrections without humiliating learners.
- Welcome beginner questions and differing technical opinions.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Prioritize safety, consent, and care for shared equipment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information, or encouraging unsafe use of another person's device are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, workshop spaces, and project communication channels. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not publish sensitive evidence in a public issue.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
